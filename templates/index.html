How CampusFix AI Works

Built around human-in-the-loop AI and transparent maintenance operations.

Duplicate Clustering

Instead of spamming staff with 20 individual reports for one leaking sink, reports are clustered with an "N students reported this" priority booster.

Safety Escalation

Critical hazards like exposed wires, gas leaks, and flooding are immediately tagged with high urgency for rapid staff triage.

Human-in-the-Loop

AI provides similarity scores and recommendations, but maintenance staff always retain ultimate authority over approvals and priority actions.

Enterprise Capabilities

Engineered for Rapid Campus Resolution

CampusFix AI bridges the gap between students, educators, and facilities dispatch teams with purpose-built features that prevent duplicate backlogs and prioritize urgent hazards.

Live Duplicate Prevention

Intelligent typeahead alerts reporters if an identical issue is already reported in their building, connecting voices instead of filing duplicate tickets.

Instant Emergency Fast-Path

Physical safety hazards like electrical sparking, gas odors, and flooding immediately trigger critical priority flags for same-hour dispatch.

End-to-End Tracking Receipts

Reporters receive a unique alphanumeric reference code and interactive 4-stage stepper, providing transparency without follow-up phone calls.

Campus Hotspot Intelligence

Building-by-building load charts empower administration to detect systemic infrastructure failures and allocate seasonal maintenance budgets proactively.
